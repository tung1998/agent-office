# J2TEAM backgrounds

The J2TEAM pack in Settings › Theme (utils/backgroundPacks.ts): public images supplied and approved for inclusion by the repository owner, contributed by Nguyễn Nam Long. Optional; existing accounts keep their current theme.

Sources: [13-image folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/10o5iq5xbUBjM2K5fW-FqtFD1jH55ke1q), [J2teaM.bmp](https://drive.google.com/file/d/0B31mZpan9psMQ0tncnREZUhrZkk/view?resourcekey=0-77nBlSGk5JgFPKeXHKLfYQ).

Images were resized with ImageMagick Lanczos to fit within 3840×2160 without cropping or changing aspect ratio, mildly sharpened (`-unsharp 0x0.7+0.45+0.02`) and encoded as WebP quality 88. The pack's video (FJ2 - DG, 54 MB) is left out of the repository.
