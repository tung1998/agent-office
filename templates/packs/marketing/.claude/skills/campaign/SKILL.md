# Campaign

## The brief (what the owner must give)
Business goal and period, the offer, who buys (the profile in `docs/brand.md`), budget range, channels the owner can run, what has been tried before. Missing: ask, at most 3 questions.

## The plan (`campaigns/<YYYY-MM>-<slug>/plan.md`)
1. **Goal**: one goal metric (leads, orders, sign-ups, booked calls) with a target and a date; secondary metrics only if they explain the goal (click rate, cost per lead).
2. **Audience**: who, their problem, the words they use (quotes from reviews, forums, comments, with links), where they are.
3. **Message**: one core promise, the proof the owner can back (results, guarantees, numbers from `docs/brand.md`), the objections and their answers, the call to action.
4. **Channels**: 1-3 channels that reach this audience, with why; per channel the format and the frequency. A small budget does fewer channels well.
5. **Calendar**: a table (date, channel, asset, goal); the assets to make.
6. **Budget**: a proposal (per channel, per week) for the owner to decide; never spent by an agent.
7. **Measurement**: for each metric, where the number comes from (which report the owner exports, which columns), the tracking needed (UTM links `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, a discount code, a form field).
8. **Risks**: what could go wrong, what the plan assumes.

## Measuring and reviewing
- Numbers only from the owner's data (exports, pasted tables, a connected read-only tool); write where each came from.
- Per campaign: goal against target, per channel and asset (reach, clicks, conversions, cost per result).
- Small numbers (under ~100 clicks or ~20 conversions per variant) are a hint, not a finding: say so.
- No cause claimed without a test (#ab-test); a correlation is a hypothesis.
- Lessons: one line each, the evidence, the channel; search `lessons` first, again = `count` + 1; `level` `hunch` → `pattern` (3 times) → `rule` (proposed to the owner for `docs/brand.md`).
