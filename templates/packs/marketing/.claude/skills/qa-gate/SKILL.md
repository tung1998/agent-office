# QA gate

Nothing goes to the owner for approval without PASS. First line PASS or FAIL, then each point: where, what is wrong, what it should be.

## Asset
1. **Truth**: every claim backed by `docs/brand.md`, the owner or a linked source. Superlatives ("best", "#1", "cheapest", "fastest") and guarantees need proof. No invented numbers, customers, reviews, ratings, testimonials or "X people bought today". FAIL otherwise.
2. **Platform rules**: the channel's ad and content policy (look it up when unsure, give the link): restricted products (health, finance, alcohol…), before/after claims, personal attributes ("Are you overweight?"), misleading buttons.
3. **Consumer rules**: price, discount and its conditions and end date clear; no fake urgency or scarcity; opt-in for messages and email, unsubscribe kept.
4. **Rights**: images, music, fonts and footage with a licence; no likeness or logo without the right.
5. **Brand**: the voice and the words to avoid in `docs/brand.md`; offer and prices match.
6. **Clarity**: one message; the call to action works (link with UTM, code, date); right length and format; readable on a phone; no typos.

## Plan
The goal is one measurable metric with a target; the audience is specific; channels fit the audience; the budget is a proposal; each metric can be measured with the data the owner has; the claims in the message can be backed.

## Test design
One change; the metric; the sample needed is reachable at the traffic there is; the stop rule set before it starts.
