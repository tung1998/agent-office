# Before posting: the checklist

Rules change: when unsure, check the current page (TikTok Community Guidelines, Branded Content Policy, the Commercial Music Library terms) on the web and give the link.

## Blocks posting (FAIL)
**Rules**
- Dangerous acts or challenges, self-harm, eating-disorder content, minors in risky or sexualised situations, hate, harassment, graphic content.
- Health, money, legal claims presented as fact without a source; "guaranteed" results; get-rich promises; medical advice beyond general information.
- Misleading content: edited to change what someone said, a fake news format, impersonation.
- Paid or gifted product shown without the paid-partnership / branded content switch; affiliate links not disclosed.
- Realistic AI-generated images, video, voices or people without the AI-generated content label; a real person's likeness or voice cloned without their consent.

**Rights**
- Every clip, image, font, logo and sound is the creator's own, royalty-free with a licence that allows commercial use, or licensed; each one listed in `assets/README.md` (or the video's `shotlist.md`).
- Music: a business account uses only TikTok's Commercial Music Library; a personal account still should not bake popular tracks into the file. Prefer picking the sound in the app when posting.
- Clips of other creators only via Duet/Stitch in the app, not re-uploaded.

**Truth**
- Each claim of fact has its source in the script's notes; numbers, prices and dates current.
- No fake reviews, testimonials, before/after, giveaways or follower counts; no "comment X to win" without real terms.

## Advice (PASS with notes)
**Craft** (skill `hooks-scripts`)
- First second: the subject is in frame and the hook is spoken and on screen.
- One idea; nothing in the middle that does not serve it; a visual change every 2-4 s.
- Text ≤ 7 words a card, inside the safe zone (skill `production`), readable on a phone.
- The ending pays off the hook; one call to action at most.
- Caption and 3-6 hashtags fit the video; no hashtag stuffing.
