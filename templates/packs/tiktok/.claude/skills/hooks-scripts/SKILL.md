# Ideas, hooks and scripts

Read `docs/channel.md` (niche, audience, voice, pillars, what the creator refuses) and the `lessons` that apply first.

## 1. Ideas (`ideas`)
One idea = one video = one point. Fields: `title`, `hook`, `angle` (what makes it this creator's take), `pillar`, `format`, `why_now` (the trend or the season, with `source` link and date), `score`, `status` `new`, `date`.

Score 1-5 on each, `score` = the sum (5-25):
- **Stop**: would the hook stop a thumb in the niche's feed?
- **Fit**: on a pillar, in the creator's voice, something they will do on camera.
- **Value**: the viewer learns, feels or gets something by the end.
- **Doable**: can be made this week with what the creator has (clips, place, skills, tools).
- **Proof**: a lesson in `lessons` or a trend with a source backs it.

## 2. Hooks (first 1-3 seconds)
The hook is spoken AND on screen AND seen (the first frame shows the subject, not a logo). Patterns that work, to adapt, never copy a creator's line:
- **Result first**: show the end result, then "here's how".
- **Call-out**: "If you [audience + situation], stop doing X."
- **Contrarian**: "Everyone says X. It's wrong for [audience]."
- **Curiosity gap**: a specific question answered by the end ("Why does … ?").
- **Number/list**: "3 [things] I wish I knew before [situation]."
- **Story open**: in the middle of the moment ("I lost 2 clients because of this email.").
Avoid: "Hey guys, welcome back", slow intros, a hook the video does not pay off (bait), ALL CAPS shouting, fake urgency.

## 3. Script (`videos/<YYYY-MM-DD>-<slug>/script.md`)
```
# <title>
Length: <15-60 s> · Format: <format> · Pillar: <pillar>

| Time | Spoken (voice) | On screen (text) | Visual / B-roll | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0-2s | hook line | hook text (≤ 7 words) | subject in frame | — |
| 2-8s | … | … | … | … |
| …    | call to action | … | … | … |

Caption: <1-2 lines, a question or a promise, ≤ 150 chars before "more">
Hashtags: <3-6: 1-2 broad of the niche, 2-3 specific, 0-1 trend>
Notes: <sources of each claim of fact; clips needed from the creator>
```
- Length: as short as the idea allows; 15-35 s for one tip, up to 60 s for a story or a tutorial. A pattern change (cut, zoom, text, angle) every 2-4 s.
- Spoken: short sentences, words the audience uses, the creator's language. On-screen text: ≤ 7 words a card, inside the safe zone (skill `production`).
- One call to action at most (follow for part 2, comment a word, save it); never ask for likes in exchange for anything.
- Claims of fact (health, money, law, products) carry a source in Notes; no source: cut it or say it as an opinion.

## 4. Repurposing (#repurpose)
From a transcript: moments of 15-60 s that make sense alone, start with a hook (or get one added as on-screen text), end on a point. Keep the meaning: never cut a sentence so it says something the speaker did not mean.
