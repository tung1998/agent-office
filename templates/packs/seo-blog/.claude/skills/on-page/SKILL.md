# On-page

## Front block (top of `articles/<slug>.md`, after the brief)
```
title:       ≤ 60 characters, the topic near the start
meta:        ≤ 155 characters, the answer and a reason to click
slug:        short-words-with-dashes
h1:          the title or close to it
schema:      Article | HowTo | FAQPage | Product (suggested; the owner adds it in the CMS)
updated:     YYYY-MM-DD
```

## Writing
- First paragraph: the direct answer to the intent.
- Then the depth: steps, examples, a table, the owner's experience (from `docs/site.md` or the owner).
- Headings that read as a table of contents; paragraphs of 2-4 sentences; lists and tables where they help.
- Facts with a link to the source and its date; prefer primary sources.
- Images: what to show and the alt text (describes the image).
- Internal links: at least 2 to existing articles, and say which existing articles should link here (the owner adds those).
- FAQ when "People also ask" asks it; each answer 2-3 sentences.
- Refresh: same URL, a short changelog at the end, `updated` date.

## The editor's checklist (PASS needs all)
1. The intent is answered in the first paragraph.
2. Covers what the top results cover, plus the brief's gap.
3. Every fact sourced; numbers right; no invented experience, reviews or statistics.
4. No copied passages (spot-check 3 sentences on the web); no spun or thin text.
5. Title, meta, H1, headings, slug, alt text within the limits.
6. Internal links in and out; FAQ and schema when relevant.
7. Keyword used naturally; no stuffing.
8. The site's voice; readable on a phone; no typos.
