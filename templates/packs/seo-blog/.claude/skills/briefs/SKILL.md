# Brief

At the top of `articles/<slug>.md`, between `<!-- brief` and `-->`:

```
keyword:          main keyword
secondary:        2-5 close keywords and questions
intent:           informational | commercial | transactional
reader:           who searches it, what they know, what they want to do next
angle:            what this article does that the top results do not (the gap)
outline:          H2/H3 list
questions:        People also ask, forum questions to answer
sources:          primary sources to cite (official, studies, docs), with links
owner input:      experience, examples, numbers only the owner has; [cần xác nhận] if missing
internal links:   existing articles to link to, and which should link here
length:           about the depth of the top results, not padded
cta:              what the reader should do next (product, signup, another article)
```

Owner input missing for a claim that needs it: ask the owner through the SEO lead, do not invent it.
