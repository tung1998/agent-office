# Keywords

## Finding them (free, with sources)
- Seeds: the owner's products, customers' questions, `docs/site.md` topics.
- Expand: the search engine's autocomplete (type the seed + a-z), "People also ask", "related searches"; forums and Q&A (Reddit, Quora, Facebook groups, Tinh tế, Voz…) for the audience's words; the owner's Search Console queries.
- Volumes: only from a named tool (Google Keyword Planner, the owner's paid tool if connected) or Search Console impressions; else write the evidence of demand (in autocomplete, asked in forums, many results) and no number.

## Reading the results page
For each keyword, open the top 5 results: kind (guide, list, product page, video, forum), depth, freshness, who writes them (big sites or small blogs), what all of them miss. The intent is what the top results serve: informational, commercial (compare, best), transactional (buy, price), navigational (a brand).

## Clusters
One pillar (broad topic, long guide) and supporting articles (one intent each), linked both ways. One intent = one article: two keywords answered by the same results page go in one article (no cannibalisation).

## Priority
high: clear demand, intent the site can serve, a gap or weak results, close to what the business sells. low: big sites own the page and no gap.

## AEO (answers in AI search)
Questions answered in the first lines, clear definitions, lists and tables, facts with sources, the author's expertise shown: the article can be quoted by AI answers. Note the questions an AI answer would pull from.

## Search Console (the owner's export)
- Performance → last 3 months compared with the previous 3 → Queries and Pages → Export.
- Low CTR with good impressions: title and meta. Position 8-20: refresh and internal links. Impressions with no matching article: a new keyword. A page falling: check freshness and what replaced it.
- Small numbers (under ~100 impressions) mean little; seasonality compared year on year when there is data.
