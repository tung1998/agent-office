# Outreach

## Shape (email under 120 words, chat under 60)
1. **Subject** (email): under 7 words, specific, no tricks ("Re:", "Fwd:" without a thread are deception).
2. **First line**: about them, a fact from a source ("Saw you opened a second shop in Thảo Điền").
3. **Why it matters**: the problem the offer solves for someone like them, one line.
4. **Proof**: one true proof from `docs/offer.md`.
5. **Ask**: one small step (reply yes, a 15-minute call this week).
6. **Sender**: real name, role, business, contact.
7. **Opt-out**: "If this is not relevant, reply 'no' and I won't write again."

## Channels
- Email: the shape above.
- LinkedIn/Zalo/Facebook: no subject, under 60 words, no link in the first message.
- In the prospect's language and register (Vietnamese: polite form of address by role).

## Law and decency
- Vietnam: Decree 91/2020 (spam): a clear sender, an easy opt-out, no messages after a no. Decree 13/2023 (personal data): business contacts from public sources, used only for this purpose.
- Abroad: CAN-SPAM (US: a real address, an opt-out honoured), GDPR (EU/UK: a legitimate interest, the source of the data on request).
- Never: invented facts, fake familiarity, false urgency, discounts or deadlines the owner did not give, testimonials not in `docs/offer.md`.
