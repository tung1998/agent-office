# Lead scoring

| Sub-score | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fit (`docs/icp.md`) | Outside | Partly | Matches | Matches the best segment |
| Need | No sign | Indirect sign | A clear sign with a source | Stated publicly (a post, a job ad, a review) |
| Timing | Nothing | Old trigger (> 6 months) | Recent (< 6 months) | This month |
| Reachable | No contact | Generic form | Company email or phone | The buyer's public work contact |

- `score` = the sum (0-12). `grade`: A ≥ 9, B 6-8, C ≤ 5.
- Each sub-score names the fact it rests on; no fact = 0. Never raise a score on a hunch.
- A leads get an `angle`: the one sentence the first message opens with (their trigger + the problem the offer solves).
- Read `lessons` with the segment's tag: a segment that never replies gets its fit lowered, with the lesson named.
